## Four long waves we must traverse

A global group of universities and research centres, coordinated by [Fondazione Comunità Messina](https://fdcmessina.org) and our own Lab, are organizing four global webinars as a way to keep our collective fingers on the pulse of the great transformations we are living through. Each webinar focuses on a large-scale systemic shift.

**Climate change** – Main speaker: Filippo Giorgi, [Intergovernmental Panel on Climate Change](https://www.ipcc.ch/). Discussant: Yıldırım Beyazıt Tuba, University of Ankara and [Mediterranean Convention on Human Rights](https://www.convenzionedeidirittinelmediterraneo.org/english). 19 November 2026, 15.00 Central European Time. **Register [here](https://us06web.zoom.us/meeting/register/XxJj1hohRPyyqXiKQTrsUA).**

**Demographic trends** – Main speaker: Massimo Livi Bacci, [International Union for the Scientific Study of Population](https://iussp.org/en). 26 November 2026, 15.00 Central European Time. Discussant: Enrique Gallicchio, [Latin American Center for a Human Economy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Latin_American_Center_for_Human_Economy) (CLAEH). **Register [here](https://us06web.zoom.us/meeting/register/A9y7G4N5TEaU0DGsSEDM7Q).**

**Political metamorphosis** – Main speaker: the late [Edgar Morin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edgar_Morin) (he recorded his lecture before his passing). 11 December 2026, 15.00 Central European Time. Discussants: Samuel Barco Serrano, [SOKIO](https://www.sokio.coop/); and Juan de Dios García Serrano, [Pablo de Olavide University](https://www.upo.es/). **Register [here](https://us06web.zoom.us/meeting/register/-T6lhMMZTT2RXlosl955pA).**

**A new economy** – Panel discussion: Olivier De Schutter, [NEEP](https://www.neep-poverty.org/), former United Nations Special Rapporteur on Extreme Poverty and Human Rights; Sandrine Dixson-Declève, [Earth4All](https://earth4all.life/); Jo Swinson, [Partners 4 a New Economy](https://p4ne.org/). 22 January 2027, 15.00 Central European Time. **Register [here](https://us06web.zoom.us/meeting/register/eRQuIZQ-QPmfCDuTu0ppsA).**

For each webinar, author [Yudhanjaya Wijeratne](../people/yudhanjaya-wijeratne/index.html) brings in relevant speculative thinking from science fiction.

Simultaneous translation into English, Spanish and Italian will be made available.

## Looking for speakers and participants

We are interested in learning from practical experiences of dealing with these great transformations. If you have such an experience and would be willing to share it, [apply here](https://workspace.scifieconomicslab.net/apps/forms/s/HQtGoFpL2pW8T5X3MtHCEcMf) to be a speaker. If you are unsure about whether your experience qualify, you will find some context and examples in [this post](https://istanbulinnovationdays.org/a-matter-of-choice-reflecting-on-alternative-economic-models-after-the-istanbul-innovation-days-2025/). We are deliberately refraining from giving hard-and-fast criteria of what constitutes experience in this context, because we believe such openness is conducive to serendipity and learning together.

If you have questions, [contact us](mailto:hello@scifieconomicslab.net).
