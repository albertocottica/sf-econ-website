---
title: Messina
---

In 2027, the Lab becomes part of a new centre for policy research and human development. It also sets up camp in Sicily, on the shores of the Messina Strait. The Lab formalizes its long-standing friendship with [Fondazione di Comunità Messina](https://fdcmessina.org/about-us/) into a long-term alliance. We become part of Eutopia, a new hub for policy research and training for human development, with headquarters in the former convent of Saint Brigid in central Messina.

The new hub is focused on place-based development. We will bring to it ideas and capabilities from new economic thinking and economic science fiction.
