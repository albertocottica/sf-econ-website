The Lab's first principle is to pay attention to the area of research at the intersection of economics and anthropology. This was spearheaded by scholars such as Marcel Mauss, Karl Polanyi, Albert O. Hirschman, James C. Scott and David Graeber. Among these authors, the vision of a “unified social science” beyond departmental borders is recurrent: the collaboration between Hirschman (an economist) and Clifford Geertz (an anthropologist) at the Princeton Institute for Advanced Study set an example for all of us in the Sci-Fi Economics Lab.
