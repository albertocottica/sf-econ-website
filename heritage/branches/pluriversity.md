The Lab's third principle is this: fundamental scientific ideas should be non-discipline specific, and instead be effective (and therefore recyclable) across different domains. Its name evokes the supposed ability of truth to infiltrate the most difficult barriers. This idea resurfaced several times in the history of science, only to find a mature expression in the Santa Fe Institute. From the SFI, the Lab derives its embrace of transdisciplinarity and eclecticism, as well as its attention to epistemology, which itself should be plural.
