Economic and human development are unevenly distributed, not just in level, but in direction and historical path, too: we have known this since Marshall pointed it out in 1890. Despite this, many economists and policy makers still struggle to incorporate spatial analysis in their thinking and action. In the Lab, we aspire to work with and for places and the communities that live there, and to contribute to the practice of place-based strategies for development.
