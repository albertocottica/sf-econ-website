Economic activity plays out _very_ differently in different places: we have known this since Marshall pointed it out in 1890. Despite this, many economists and policy makers still struggle to incorporate spatial analysis in their thinking and action. In the Lab, we want to work with regions, cities, towns and remote areas, and try to learn from all of them in the quest for a better understanding of the role of place in economic development.
