While most science fiction is about possible technological futures, some of it concerns itself directly to alternative economic and social systems, which are realistic enough to give the reader pause. We see it as a way to break the chain of economic imagination: Fredric Jameson quipped that it is easier to imagine the end of the world than the end of capitalism, but this is not so true... if you read enough science fiction!
