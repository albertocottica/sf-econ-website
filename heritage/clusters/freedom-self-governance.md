Individual and collective freedoms in modern moral philosophy are considered the appropriate moral foundation for societies, hence economies. These are not the formal freedoms of neoliberals, but substantive ones: Amartya Sen called them "capabilities". In a well-run society, more freedom means more initiatives, so more development.
