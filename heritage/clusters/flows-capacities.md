why: "Economic activity as flows of materials, energy and money, and the capacities — natural, built and human — that set their limits."
summary: "While economies are best understood as the institutions, rules and norms around cooperative production, it's useful to consider the economy in the context of flows. Wood, wool and metal are crafted into a chair in which someone can sit and read a book. Flows can be material or energetic, circular or unidirectional. Money accounting can make these flows explicit, and serve as a diagnostic for when accumulation impedes flow."
