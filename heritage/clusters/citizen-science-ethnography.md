There is a story that says this ordinary people do not understand economics. Hence, decisions about the economy should be made not by everybody, but only by those who do understand it, Christine Lagarde's "adults in the room". Democracy, then does not apply to economic policy. This is just false: like Karl Polanyi wrote, common people understand their economic well-being much better than any expert, and their perspective on economic problems is precious to diagnose them (for example, the imbalances hidden in how inflation is measured). Even in the hard science, the contribution of non-scientists to scientific research is highly prized. Participatory ethnography and citizen science are used as devices through which non-economists can contribute to economic analysis and policy.
