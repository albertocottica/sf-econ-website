Additionally, development itself is a politically contested concept: Global North countries insist that Global South ones should develop by opening up their markets to foreign competition, while they themselves made strong use of infant industry protection. So, when trying to help a territory to develop, ask yourself: what does 'development' mean _in this specific context, for these specific people_? How can you enhance their agency on the way they do it?"
